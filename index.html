Music energyAuto follows genre tags when apps provide them. Drift, Flow and Pulse are always available.
Real audio reactionsOff · The iris uses its usual music animation

Optional and started only by you. Android asks for an audio permission (labeled microphone) and a full-screen capture confirmation. EYES uses that approval for playback sound only. It does not open your microphone, inspect screen images, or save or upload audio. Keep the music playing, approve the prompt, then close The Optic to see the Eye react. Stop anytime, including from Android's notification. Capture ends when you leave EYES. Some music apps block playback capture; the usual iris animation keeps working.

Two Eyes on two phones. No messages, words or feeds. Just a signal sent across the distance.

Hold two fingers on the iris to send a pulse. Answer each other’s pulse quickly and the two Eyes resonate.

Opt-in. No messages or personal activity are shared. Signals are encrypted on your phone. A free third-party relay (ntfy.sh) carries the ciphertext and can see connection metadata. Only someone with your secret code can join. Signals arrive while EYES is open; short-lived ones may wait for you to come back. End Eye Contact at any time.
